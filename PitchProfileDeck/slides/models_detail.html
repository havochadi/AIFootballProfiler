About 60 seconds, a companion to the previous slide. Read down the table, not every row: the first column is the model, the second is its one job, the third is what goes in and what comes out, and the last says whether we trained it. Pretrained means we use it as released: the YOLOv8x detectors, the HRNet pitch network, and the ResNet-34 plus PARSeq number reader. No training means it is a method, not a learned model: the BoT-SORT tracker with its camera-cut detector, and K-means on shirt colour. We fine-tuned two: the CLIP identity model on about six million player thumbnails, and the T-DEED action spotter on SoccerNet and FOOTPASS clips. The event rules are hand-written and turn positions into passes, carries, sprints and distance.
