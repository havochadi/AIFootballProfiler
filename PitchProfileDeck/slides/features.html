About 30 seconds. These four are exactly what the live demo shows, in this order: the match report, the player profiles, the event maps and the live tracking on the real video. The line at the bottom is the archetype profiler: players are rated against 42 playing styles, and a model estimates the styles of players nobody has rated. It is built, but nobody has labelled players yet, so it is not part of the demo.
