What you can do in the app

Six things the app gives you

Upload and check

Length, resolution and format are checked. At least 10 minutes; a full half is best.

Match report

Possession, passes, shots and pressures for both teams, plus standout players.

Player profiles

Heatmap, distance, speed zones, passing, defending and off-ball runs for each player.

Event maps

Twelve event types as routes or heatmaps, filtered by team or player.

Live tracking

The system’s boxes drawn over the original footage, beside the same players on the pitch.

Archetype profiling

Rate players against 42 roles; a semi-supervised model estimates the rest.

About 60 seconds, then switch to the live demo. The first five cards are what the demo shows: upload with length checks, the match report, player profiles, event maps and the live tracking overlay on the real footage. The sixth card is the archetype profiler: players are rated as percentages across 42 roles, and a semi-supervised model (label spreading) estimates the roles of unlabelled players. It is built but not yet fitted, because no players have been labelled yet, so it is not part of the demo. Be upfront about that.
