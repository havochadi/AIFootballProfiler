A shot

Pixels

An 8-second clip of 100 frames goes into the T-DEED video model.

Score

It outputs a score per frame and class, with the team. A score ≥ 0.575 is a shot.

Link

The shooter is that team’s release within 1.5 s, else the player nearest the ball.

Name

About 75 seconds. Three concrete traces from pixels to a number. A pass: the detector boxes people and ball, the pitch model gives them coordinates, then a rule says that if the ball leaves a player and a teammate touches it within four seconds in the same camera view, it is a complete pass; an opponent means intercepted. The pass is credited to the passer once identity names the tracklet. A run: foot positions in metres are smoothed over one second and measured in 0.4 second steps, with steps above 11 metres per second discarded as tracking errors; distance is the sum, top speed is the 98th percentile so one spike cannot set it, and a sprint is a second or more above 25.2 kilometres per hour. A shot is different: no rule works, so a learned video model watches an eight-second clip and scores each frame; a confident score becomes a shot, credited to the nearest release by that team. The shot precision and recall are measured against SoccerNet labels on held-out halves.
