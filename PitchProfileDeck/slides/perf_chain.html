About 55 seconds. This is the whole system, video in and credited event out, on three unseen FOOTPASS games with the true events and true players for reference. Pale bar: the event was found. Mid green: it was also credited to a named player. Dark green: credited to the right named player. For passes, 90.0% are found, but only 41.0% are credited to a named player and 34.3% credited to the right one. Why the drop: crediting needs three things to line up at the same moment, the ball, the right track, and a name for that track, and only about half of player time is named. When a credit is made it is mostly right: 83.7% for passes and 89.9% for carries. For rare events the loss starts earlier: only 3.8% of tackles are found at all (1 of 26 in these games). The practical consequence is on the slide: team totals count every found event for the team and so use only the pale bar; player totals use the dark bar, so they are lower and should be read as relative comparisons. Models: this is the whole chain, the YOLOv8x detector, BoT-SORT tracker, CLIP identity model and T-DEED spotter, plus the crediting logic.
