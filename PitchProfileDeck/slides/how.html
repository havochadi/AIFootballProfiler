PitchProfile · AAI3001

4

About 75 seconds. Walk the three lanes. Lane A is perception: sample the video, detect everything, track within each camera shot and map pixels to a 105 by 68 metre pitch. Detection is the only GPU-heavy step and its output is saved, so every later stage can be re-run on the CPU. Lane B is the football logic: split teams by kit colour, find one ball path, then derive passes, carries and pressures with hand-written rules, while a learned video model (T-DEED, which we fine-tuned) spots shots, tackles, blocks and headers that rules cannot tell apart. Lane C is identity and counting: read shirt numbers, use a fine-tuned CLIP model to link the segments without a readable number, then compute statistics per player. The colours show where each model comes from: blue is pretrained and used as is, green we trained or fine-tuned, grey is not a learned model. Count: nine learned models in the pipeline are the YOLOv8x player detector, the YOLOv8x ball detector, HRNet, ResNet-34, PARSeq, CLIP, T-DEED, the shot classifier and the re-identification head; the next slides explain them.
