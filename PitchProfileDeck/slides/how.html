Backup · How it works

Four steps from video to statistics

1 · See

Find every player and the ball in each frame.

2 · Place

Turn camera pixels into metres on the pitch.

3 · Identify

Team from kit colour. Player from shirt number.

4 · Count

Passes, shots, distance and runs from the movement.

Only the video is used. No data feeds. A line-up is optional and not used for any number shown.

PitchProfile · AAI3001

22

About 60 seconds. Walk left to right. See: the AI finds each player and the ball in every frame. Place: it finds the pitch lines, which lets us measure real distance and speed in metres instead of pixels. Identify: the two teams are told apart by kit colour, and each player by the number on his shirt. Count: once we know who is where, passes, shots, distance and runs follow from the movement and the ball. The point to land: the video is the only input, nothing is bought from a data provider.
