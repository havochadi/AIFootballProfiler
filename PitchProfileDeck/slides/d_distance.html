How far and how fast did each player run?

The problem

Raw pitch positions jitter by decimetres, which at 12.5 samples a second looks like fast running. Summing them directly would overstate distance and invent sprints.

Our answer

Hand-written

Smoothing and step rules

Data it uses: Each player’s pitch positions in metres, 12.5 a second.

Trained on: Nothing. Hand-written, no training data.

How it works

1

Smooth positions over 13 samples, about one second.

2

Measure steps every 0.4 s; discard steps over 11 m/s as errors; sum into distance.

3

A sprint is 3 steps in a row at 25.2 km/h or faster.

Outcome

Per player

distance, speed zones, sprints

Counts cover only the time a player was on screen, so team totals are fuller than player totals.

Top speed is the 98th percentile of step speeds. Step 4: A sprint is 3 steps in a row at 25.2 km/h or faster, counted once. A high-intensity run is 5.5 m/s for at least 1 s.
