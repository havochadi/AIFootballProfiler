What it does

A broadcast match goes in. A profile of every player comes out.

1 · Upload

Any broadcast match video, from 10 minutes up to a full half.

2 · Analyse

Tracks every player, maps them onto the pitch and finds the events.

3 · Report

Team comparison, per-player statistics, event maps and the tracking itself.

About 45 seconds. The idea: you give it a normal TV broadcast of a match and it returns what a scout or analyst would want: how far each player ran, where they played, how many passes, interceptions and shots. The numbers on this slide are from the demo match we are about to show: 36 player tracks profiled, 1,202 events placed on the pitch, about 90 statistics per player, and 33 minutes of compute for a 45-minute half. Nothing comes from a data provider: the video is the only input.
