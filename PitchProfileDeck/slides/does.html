About 30 seconds. Say it in one sentence: you give it a TV broadcast of a match, and it tells you how each player played: how far they ran, where they were, how many passes and shots. The three numbers are from the demo match, Chelsea against Manchester United: 22 players with at least 10 minutes on screen, 2,505 events placed on the pitch, and about 70 minutes of computing for the 95-minute video. Nothing comes from a data provider. The video is the only input.
