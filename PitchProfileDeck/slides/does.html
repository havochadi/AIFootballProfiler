About 45 seconds. Walk the four phases left to right; the live demo follows the first three. Phase 1, upload: you add a match video. The app checks its length (a full half or a full match), size and picture quality, and estimates how long the analysis will take. Phase 2, analyse: the models run. They follow every player and the ball, place them on the pitch in metres, read shirt numbers to know who is who, and find the events such as passes and shots. This takes about three quarters of the video's length on one RTX 3090. Phase 3, report: you get a match overview, a profile for each player, maps of every event type, and the tracking drawn over the original video. Phase 4, review, is optional: you can correct a player's name, and the app re-runs the statistics with your correction. Nothing but the video is used as input.
