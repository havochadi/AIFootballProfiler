How do we name a player whose number is hidden?

The problem

A number can be read for only 38% of tracklets. Team-mates wear the same kit, and goalkeepers wear a kit that matches neither team, so colour alone does not separate players.

Our answer

Fine-tuned by us

CLIP ViT-B/16 identity model

Data it uses: 224 × 224 thumbnails of each tracked player.

Trained on: 48 FOOTPASS games: 6.0 million thumbnails of 1,485 players with true numbers, identities and goalkeeper flags.

How it works

1

One CLIP ViT-B/16 network takes a 224 × 224 thumbnail.

2

Two digit heads read the tens and units digits separately.

3

An appearance head groups one player’s thumbnails; a goalkeeper head flags keepers.

Outcome

98.3%

of tracklets matched to the right player

Goalkeepers found with 89.9% recall at 99.0% precision. Learning digits only from readable thumbnails lifted the right-number rate from 74% to 88%.

Learning digits only from readable thumbnails lifted the right-number rate from 74% to 88%. More detail. Step 1: A CLIP vision transformer takes a 224 × 224 thumbnail, scaled to fit and padded with grey because players are tall and narrow. Step 2: Digit heads: the tens digit and the units digit are read separately, so a number never seen in training can still be read. Step 3: Appearance head: a 256-number code. Thumbnails of one player are pulled together, and every other player of the same game, team-mates included, is pushed away. Step 4: Goalkeeper head: goalkeepers wear a kit that matches neither team, so colour alone often misses them. Origin: OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/16 weights, fine-tuned by us. Trained on: 48 FOOTPASS games: 6.0 million thumbnails of 1,485 players, with true numbers, identities and goalkeeper flags. Loss: Cross-entropy on both digits, contrastive loss on appearance, binary cross-entropy for goalkeeper. Settings: AdamW, 1e-5 for the backbone and 2e-4 for the new heads, 10,000 steps on one RTX 3090. Key trick: Digits are learned only from readable thumbnails. Without it the digit heads learn to guess from appearance: the right-number rate rose from 74% to 88% of grouped player time.
