What it means

For passes, about 9 in 10 are found and about 9 in 10 of the calls are real. Tackles are mostly missed (7 of 28) and mostly invented (24 of 31 calls), so they are shown as expected counts.

About 55 seconds. This is the T-DEED video action spotter, fine-tuned by us, on two games it never saw, 3,980 labelled actions. Left: for each event type, precision (of what it called, how much was real) and recall (of what really happened, how much it found), at the threshold that gives the best F1 for that type. Right: the same thing as counts. For passes, 1,721 real, 1,509 found, 192 invented and 212 missed. Carries: 1,449 real, 1,247 found. Shots: 44 real, 31 found, 23 invented, 13 missed. Tackles: 28 real, 7 found, 24 invented, 21 missed, average precision just 0.10. Average precision is the threshold-free score: 1.0 is perfect. Fine-tuning on our data lifted mean average precision across all 12 event types from 0.606 to 0.628. Say honestly that the best-F1 threshold per type was picked on these same games, so precision and recall are slightly flattering; average precision is not affected. Because tackles and blocks are weak, the app shows them as expected counts, not totals. Model: T-DEED, a video action spotter, fine-tuned on SoccerNet Ball Action Spotting and FOOTPASS.
