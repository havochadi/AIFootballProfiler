What it means

The rules reconstruct passes and carries well from positions. Per-player pass totals stay close to the truth (r = 0.89) even though 3 in 10 single passes are missed or wrong.

About 50 seconds. This tests the event rules alone, on annotated positions from one SoccerTrack match, so no vision errors are mixed in. Each bar is a whole set of real events: dark green found, grey missed, orange invented calls that matched nothing. Passes: of 1,069 real passes, 744 found with the right player within one second; 354 invented. That is 68% precision and 70% recall. Carries are similar. Ball wins, tackles and blocks are mostly wrong because a tackle looks like a touch in position data. The top-left number: correlation 0.89 between our per-player pass counts and the real ones across 56 player-halves, which is why players can be compared on passing even though individual passes are not perfect. Right: shots. Shots are not found by rules: the video spotter finds them. On four unseen halves with 58 labelled shots, the spotter finds 42 and calls 20 that were not real. Rules: the event rules are hand-written, with no learning; shots come from the video spotter.
