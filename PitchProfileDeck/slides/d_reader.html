About 60 seconds. Can we read the number on a shirt? The problem: A shirt number names the player, but in most thumbnails it cannot be read: the player faces the camera, is far away or is turned. Reading every one would produce confident wrong numbers. Our answer: ResNet-34 legibility check + PARSeq reader (used as released). Data it uses: The torso crop of each tracked player, as a thumbnail. Trained on: SoccerNet jersey-number images, from the original authors. Used as released. How it works: Cut the torso from the player box and scale it to a thumbnail. A ResNet-34 decides whether a number is readable at all. PARSeq reads the digits. Readings from one track are combined into one answer. Training detail: Not trained by us. The SoccerNet jersey-number pipeline (Koshkina and Elder; PARSeq by Bautista and Atienza), used as released. Outcome: 80.3% of 1,211 tracklets right. Counting “not visible” as right. 76.8% of the tracklets with a readable number are read correctly. More detail. Step 1: The player box is cut to the torso band and scaled to a thumbnail. Step 2: A ResNet-34 legibility classifier decides whether a number is readable at all. Most thumbnails are not: the player faces the camera, is far away or is turned. Step 3: PARSeq, a scene-text recogniser, reads the digits from the readable thumbnails. Step 4: Readings from the evenly spaced thumbnails of one tracklet are combined into one answer: a number, or “not visible”. In → out: A thumbnail → readable or not, and the digits. Origin: The SoccerNet jersey-number pipeline (Koshkina and Elder; PARSeq by Bautista and Atienza), used as released. Measured: 80.3% of 1,211 tracklets right, counting “not visible” as right. 76.8% of readable tracklets read right. Today: The legibility classifier also decides which thumbnails may vote for the identity model. PARSeq is a second opinion on every name: without it the share of visible time named correctly falls from 52.3% to 44.0%. Note: Tracklets with no readable number are correctly called “not visible” 88.7% of the time.
