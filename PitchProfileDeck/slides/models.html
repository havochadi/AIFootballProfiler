The AI behind each step

One model family for each job

1 · See

YOLOv8x detector and BoT-SORT tracker

Finds players and follows them between frames.

2 · Place

HRNet pitch-keypoint network

Finds the pitch lines, so positions are in metres.

3 · Identify

CLIP vision model and shirt-number reader

Tells players apart and reads numbers. We fine-tuned it.

4 · Count

Simple rules and the T-DEED video model

Rules count passes. The video model spots shots and tackles. We fine-tuned it.

Everything runs on one local GPU. No cloud AI service.

About 60 seconds. One card per step, same order as the last slide. See: YOLOv8x finds people and the ball in each frame, and BoT-SORT follows them from frame to frame. Place: an HRNet network finds 57 key points on the pitch lines. Identify: a CLIP vision transformer, which we fine-tuned on about six million player thumbnails, reads shirt numbers and tells team-mates apart. Count: passes and carries come from simple rules over positions and the ball; shots, headers and blocks come from the T-DEED video model, which we also fine-tuned. Two of the four are our own training work: the identity model and the action spotter. Not shown: the archetype profiler (label spreading over a nearest-neighbour graph) and a shot classifier used as a fallback.
