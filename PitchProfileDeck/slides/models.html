About 45 seconds, then the three deep-dive slides. This groups every model by where it comes from, because that decides how much we can trust it and how much work it took. Pretrained: the detector, pitch-keypoint network and shirt-number reader are strong published models that we use as released. Fine-tuned here: the two that matter most for this project. The T-DEED action spotter starts from the SoccerNet 2025 baseline and was retrained on football video for shots, tackles, blocks and headers; the CLIP vision transformer was fine-tuned on about six million player thumbnails so it can read digits, tell teammates apart and spot goalkeepers. Trained here: three small models built from our own pipeline output: the fallback shot classifier, the re-identification head, and the archetype profiler, which is built but not fitted because no players are labelled yet. Not learned: tracking, team split, ball path and the football rules. We kept those classical because they are cheap, easy to debug and measurable.
