About 60 seconds. Do not read the table; pick three rows. The detector and the pitch-keypoint network are strong pretrained models we use as they are. The two we trained ourselves matter most: a CLIP vision transformer fine-tuned on about six million player thumbnails so it can read shirt numbers and tell teammates apart, and the T-DEED action spotter, fine-tuned for shots, headers and blocks. Tracking is classical BoT-SORT with camera-motion compensation, not a learned model in this setup. The archetype profiler uses label spreading over a nearest-neighbour graph of player statistics, which is how we use only partial labels. NBJW is No-Bells-Just-Whistles. The shot classifier (gradient-boosted trees) is a fallback and is left off the slide.
