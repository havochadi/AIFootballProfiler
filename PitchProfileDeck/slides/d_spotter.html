About 60 seconds. How do we find passes, shots and tackles? The problem: Some events leave little trace in positions: a tackle looks like any other touch, and the ball is often missed. The video itself shows what happened, so the model has to watch it. Our answer: T-DEED action spotter (fine-tuned by us). Data it uses: 8-second video clips: 100 frames at 12.5 a second, 796 × 448 px. Trained on: SoccerNet ball-action games plus 48 FOOTPASS games, with action labels. How it works: Watch 8-second clips: 100 frames at 12.5 a second. An image network per frame and a temporal encoder-decoder place each action on the right frame. Heads give one of 12 actions, its exact moment and the team. Overlapping clips are merged. Training detail: Fine-tuned by us from the SoccerNet 2025 T-DEED baseline on ball-action games plus 48 FOOTPASS games: 3,000 steps, about 3 hours. Outcome: 0.628 mean average precision, unseen games. Pass 0.93, carry 0.90, shot 0.66, tackle 0.10. Fine-tuning raised it from 0.606. More detail. Step 1: The model watches an 8-second clip: 100 frames at 12.5 frames a second, 796 × 448 pixels. Step 2: A small image network (RegNet-Y 200MF with gate-shift modules) makes a feature for every frame, mixing information between neighbouring frames. Step 3: A temporal encoder-decoder sharpens the differences between frames, so an action can be placed on the right frame. Step 4: Three heads read the result: which of 12 actions, how far the true moment is from the frame, and which team did it. Step 5: Clips overlap by three quarters. Overlapping predictions are merged (soft non-maximum suppression) and scores below 0.05 are dropped. Origin: SoccerNet 2025 team ball-action baseline, fine-tuned by us: 3,000 steps, about 3 hours. Credit: Team from the screen side; for tackles and blocks, from the previous touch. Player: whoever’s box holds the ball at that moment. Counting: Expected counts: the sum of scores, not events above a threshold. Measured: Mean average precision 0.628. Pass 0.93, carry 0.90, shot 0.66, tackle 0.10. Result: Fine-tuning raised mean average precision from 0.606 to 0.628 on two unseen games.
