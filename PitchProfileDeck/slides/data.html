5

About 60 seconds. This is the same pipeline seen from the data's point of view. Read down the left column: the raw video is just pixels, 67,500 frames for a 45-minute half. We keep every second frame. The detector turns each frame into a list of boxes, which is the first time anything is a thing rather than a pixel. The tracker gives boxes a persistent ID, but only within one camera shot because the broadcast cuts every 3.5 seconds on average. The pitch model gives every foot point a coordinate in metres, which is what makes distance and speed meaningful. From positions and the ball path we derive events: typed, timed and placed on the pitch. Identity then joins tracklets into named players, and only at that point can events be added up per player into statistics. The right-hand pills show which model, if any, does each step.
