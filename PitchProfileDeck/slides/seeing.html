YOLOv8x detector

Pretrained · Roboflow football

What it is

One-stage object detector, run at 1280 px so a distant player or ball stays visible.

In → out

A frame in; a box, class (player, goalkeeper, referee, ball) and confidence for each object out.

How we use it

Every sampled frame. A ball-only YOLOv8x re-runs where no ball was found.

Why it matters

The only GPU-heavy step. Boxes are saved, so later stages re-run without it.

BoT-SORT tracker

Not a learned model here

What it is

Links boxes across frames using predicted motion. Camera motion is compensated by sparse optical flow; appearance matching is off.

Camera cuts

A jump in the colour histogram (distance > 0.35) ends all tracks. No identity crosses a cut.

In → out

Boxes per frame in; tracklets with persistent IDs out.

Why it matters

Cuts every ~3.5 s make tracklets short; identity must reconnect them.

HRNetV2-W48 pitch model

Pretrained · No-Bells-Just-Whistles

What it is

High-resolution network that predicts 57 pitch keypoints (line crossings, circle, boxes), in half precision.

In → out

A frame in; keypoints out, fitted by RANSAC to a pixel-to-pitch homography with a white-line check.

How we use it

Every second sample; outliers dropped, gaps inside a shot interpolated, result smoothed.

Why it matters

Gives distance, speed and heatmaps in metres. Only 54% of the demo half showed a usable view.

PitchProfile · AAI3001

8

About 60 seconds. Three models that turn pixels into positions. The YOLOv8x detector is the workhorse: it runs at 1280 pixels on every sampled frame because players and especially the ball are tiny in a wide broadcast shot. It outputs boxes with a class, and a second ball-only model re-runs on frames where the first found no ball. BoT-SORT is not a learned model in our setup: it predicts where each box should be next, with the camera motion estimated from optical flow, and links detections. When the broadcast cuts, detected by a jump in the colour histogram, every track ends and nothing is carried across. The HRNet pitch model finds 57 landmarks on the pitch markings; we fit a homography to them, check it against the white lines, drop outliers and smooth it. That is what converts pixels to metres. All of the downstream statistics depend on this step, and it limits us: only 54% of the demo half showed enough pitch for a calibration.
