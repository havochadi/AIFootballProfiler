This is the honest slide, and the demo shows the first one directly: in the Live tracking tab the boxes disappear whenever the broadcast cuts away, and only about half of this match showed a usable view of the pitch. Second, the ball is tiny and often hidden, so we cannot always say who passed it. Third, a tackle looks like any other touch if you only have positions, so we use a video model, and even that finds roughly one tackle in eight; we report them as expected counts, not totals. Fourth, naming players: about half of player time is tied to a shirt number, and striped kits are the hardest to read. Also worth one sentence if asked: no player has been labelled for the archetype model yet, the best training data is under a SoccerNet NDA, and one full match takes about 70 minutes on one GPU.
