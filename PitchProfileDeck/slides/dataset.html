About 60 seconds. This is the research gap behind the project. Public datasets each cover one piece of the job. SoccerNet Ball Action Spotting gives twelve ball actions with times. FOOTPASS, released under an NDA, gives every player’s box, shirt number and team, plus eight action types. SoccerNet Jersey gives shirt numbers, SoccerNet tracking gives player and ball boxes, and SoccerTrack gives positions, the ball and every action for one match. We had to combine all five to build one pipeline, and each was used for a different model.
