4 · Scalability

Scaling up: more matches, better models

Scale

More matches

Each match is processed on its own, about 0.75× its length on one RTX 3090, so a 90-minute match takes about 70 minutes. Only broadcast video is needed, so any match on video can be added.

Improve

Better models

More training data helped: moving the identity model from 19 to 48 games raised the right-name share from 48.9% to 53.0%. The ball is still the weakest link: it is found in under half of the frames where it is visible.

Next

Name more players

About half of visible player time is still unnamed. In a test with the true squads, a line-up raised the right-name share from 53.0% to 64.2%; more thumbnails and the naming panel also give names, and unnamed players keep their statistics.

Possible extension

Player grading and playing styles

A reviewer would grade players against a list of styles, and a model would estimate the rest. Nobody has graded players yet, so it needs labels before it can be tested. Our player statistics would be its input.
