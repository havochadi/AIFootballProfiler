About 45 seconds, then take questions. The first three fix measurement quality, in the order we expect them to help most. The ball is the biggest lever: about a quarter of the passes the video model finds cannot be given to a player because no ball was found at that moment. Naming players: reading more shirt numbers, and letting a reviewer name the rest. Tackles: the data does not exist yet, so we need many more labelled examples. The fourth is finding the ball more often: it is found in under half of the frames where it is visible, and every pass and shot depends on it. The line at the bottom is product work: today the full-match pipeline analyses matches that are already in the library, and the demo shows the intended upload flow, so be clear that arbitrary upload is future work.
