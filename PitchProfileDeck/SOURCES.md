## How statistics help clubs

| Claim | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruitment: clubs use video and data to evaluate players, find overlooked talent in external datasets and profile strengths and weaknesses | Frost, W., Groom, R. and Nicholls, S. B. (2025). | Tactics: tracking and event data support tactical analysis, replacing expert observation that is subjective and time-consuming | Rein, R. and Memmert, D. (2016). [Big data and tactical analysis in elite soccer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4996805/). *SpringerPlus* 5, 1410 | Opened. Peer-reviewed review article, open access. |
| Workload: GPS data are used to monitor training load for each player and the team, linked to injury risk | Ravé, G., Granacher, U., Boullosa, D., Hackney, A. C. and Zouhal, H. (2020). An opinion paper; the workload-to-injury link it relies on (acute:chronic ratio) is debated in the literature. |
| Example: Brentford uses data alongside scouting to find players whose potential exceeds their price | [ESPN, "Brentford close in on Premier League dream. Just don't thank 'Moneyball' for their success"](https://www.espn.com/soccer/english-league-championship/story/4145973/brentford-close-in-on-premier-league-dream-just-dont-thank-moneyball-for-their-success); [Sky Sports, "Brentford, Brighton, Bournemouth: how the Premier League's 'Bermuda Triangle' defies the odds"](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13590434/brentford-brighton-bournemouth-how-the-premier-leagues-bermuda-triangle-defies-the-odds-with-recruitment-and-culture) | Search extracts. ## "Not a new idea"

| Claim | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tracking from broadcast video is already sold | [SkillCorner, XY tracking data](https://skillcorner.com/products/football/xy-tracking-data) | Opened. Company claims (150+ competitions, tracking from a single camera). |
| Broadcast-derived tracking came close to stadium cameras for most events | Mills, K. et al. (2026). [Automatic event detection in association football using broadcast-derived tracking data](https://shura.shu.ac.uk/37410/). *Sports Engineering* 29(1) | Opened (abstract). Independent and peer reviewed. One 2022 World Cup match, compared with TRACAB Gen 5 and FIFA's manually tagged events; best case matched or was within 0.05 F1 for most events, limited by player visibility, ball tracking errors and subjective event definitions. |

## Claims deliberately not used

- Analyst hours per match, and a 10-15% tagging difference between analysts: could not be traced to a reliable source.
- Anything about cost: only unattributed search snippets were found.
- SkillCorner's "98% accuracy" claim: not on the company's own product page.
